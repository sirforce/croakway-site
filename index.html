Reggorf

An original lane-crossing arcade game for iPhone. Hop the road, ride the river, and get all five frogs home.

Support

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue and include your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. Beta testers can also send feedback from TestFlight.

How to play

  • Swipe to hop in any direction. A long swipe hops twice.
  • Tap to hop forward.
  • Cross the road, ride logs and lily pads, and land in each of the five homes.
  • You have 30 seconds per frog.
  • The speaker button turns sound on or off.
