Reggorf Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

Reggorf does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information.

  • The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.
  • The game does not connect to the internet.
  • Your high score and sound setting are saved only on your device and are removed when you delete the app.
  • If you install a beta through TestFlight, Apple may collect crash and usage data and share it with the developer under Apple's own privacy policy. Any feedback you choose to send through TestFlight is used only to improve the game.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address.
